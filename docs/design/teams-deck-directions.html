Teams Deck Directions

Five directions for the teams deck

Same copy, same light ground, same twelve slides. What changes is the visual system: type, layout, how the accent is spent, and how the numbers are drawn. Each direction shows the cover, the guarantee slide, and the pricing slide, so the three hardest slides are compared like-for-like.

Click any slide to see it full size. Arrow keys move between slides, Esc closes. Directions 6 and 7 are the two hybrids you asked for: Swiss and Blueprint, each inside the Dashboard frame.

Swiss

Keeps the house typeface and strips everything else back: a strict grid, big tight-set type, thin black rules instead of cards, and the accent spent once per slide as a short bar. The Stripe-and-Linear school of restraint. Closest to what's live, so the cheapest to adopt.

Type
Geist only (the site's current face)
Ground
Pure white, rules not cards
Accent
One teal bar and one teal word per slide
Build effort
Low: spacing and type sizes, no new fonts

How to pick

Say the number or name, including 6 or 7 for the hybrids. I will rebuild all twelve slides in that direction on the live page, keeping every word as it is, and then we go through the content slide by slide. Mixing is fine too, for example Swiss type with the Dashboard pricing table.
