Read the ledger
  • Pickup is the constraint. Move it before you touch talk tracks.
  • Connect→set holds steady across pods — the setting skill is not the problem.
  • Dials/set is your true cost per set. Lower is better.
  • Show rate belongs to setters and confirmations, never to closers.

Two things to keep straight. Rounding compounds down the chain, so a big scale-up can land a unit or two off a hand calc — directional, not penny-exact. And "on calendar" and "showed" come from a different cohort than this week's sets, so set→calendar is directional only until every set carries a date-set and a date-scheduled field in the CRM.
