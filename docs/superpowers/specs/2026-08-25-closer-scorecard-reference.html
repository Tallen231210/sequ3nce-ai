Three things to keep straight. Booked-call close rate is the KPI — live close rate is diagnostic only, because a closer can flatter it by hiding behind no-shows. Show rate on first calls belongs to your setters and confirmation motion, not to the closer; the only show rate a closer owns is FU show, on calls they booked themselves. And rounding compounds down the chain, so a big scale-up lands a unit or two off a hand calc — directional, not penny-exact. Nothing saves; reload to start over.
